# Coffee UX test

Interactive coffee checkout prototype matching the cart mockup.

Quantity controls, recommendations, nearest pickup selected by default, and payment through a new SBP account or a new card. The cafe card is informational. Points cover up to 10% after discounts, limited by the available balance; accrual is 5% of the amount paid, rounded down to whole points.

No real payments or orders.

https://asushkova.github.io/coffee-ux-test/
